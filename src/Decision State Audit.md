# Decision State Audit

Evaluate whether a decision should be made under the current human state.

---

## Core Idea

Failures often occur not because of wrong actions,
but because actions are taken under invalid decision states.

---

## Focus

- Detect combined risks across systems
- Identify improper sequencing of actions
- Reveal responsibility that should not be individually assumed

---

## Principles

- Do not provide solutions
- Do not optimize outcomes
- Only determine whether the decision state is valid

---

## Output

- High-risk concentration points
- Actions that should be delayed or avoided
- Decisions requiring additional confirmation

---

## Anti-Goals

- No decision-making on behalf of users
- No execution guidance
- No guarantee of correctness

---

> The most dangerous action is a correct decision made at the wrong time.
