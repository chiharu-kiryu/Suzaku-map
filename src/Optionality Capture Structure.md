# Optionality Capture Structure

Capture future uncertain value through low-cost, trust-bound relationships.

---

## Core Idea

Instead of optimizing for immediate returns,
establish structures that secure optional access
to future high-uncertainty outcomes.

---

## Principles

- Prioritize optionality over short-term efficiency
- Bind access, not ownership
- Invest in asymmetric potential, not proven value
- Minimize control to maximize long-term viability

---

## Mechanism

- Identify under-recognized or mispriced potential
- Provide minimal survival or continuity support
- Establish future participation rights (not obligations)
- Maintain low-interference relationships

---

## Boundaries

- No forced productivity
- No short-term ROI pressure
- No full ownership or control
- No dependency creation

---

## Value

- Positions the system at high-variance future nodes
- Preserves access without heavy capital commitment
- Enables long-term asymmetric upside

---

> Control reduces optionality; access preserves it.
