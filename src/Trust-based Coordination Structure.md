# Trust-based Coordination Structure

Enable collaboration without reliance on centralized systems.

---

## Core Idea

In unstable or low-trust environments,
formal systems (employment, education, institutions)
fail to provide reliable coordination.

Small-scale, trust-based structures can replace them
for collaboration and survival.

---

## Principles

- Trust over formal verification
- Small-scale over scalability
- Optional participation over binding contracts
- Coordination without institutional dependency

---

## Mechanism

- Pre-existing trust or verified interaction history
- Low-visibility, private operation
- Task-based collaboration (not role-based)
- Flexible entry and exit

---

## Boundaries

- No mass onboarding
- No platformization
- No ideological alignment enforcement
- No dependency on a central authority

---

## Value

- Maintains coordination under system instability
- Reduces reliance on broken institutions
- Preserves autonomy while enabling collaboration

---

> Coordination survives where systems fail.
