# Low Cognitive Load Pipeline

Design workflows that minimize cognitive load and decision-making overhead, even at the cost of optimality.

---

## Principles

- Prefer consistency over optimization
- Avoid decision points during execution
- Use templates to eliminate variability
- Keep tools replaceable and simple
- Optimize for "not thinking", not "best outcome"

---

## Use Cases

- Repetitive production tasks
- Maintenance workflows
- Low-stakes continuous output systems

---

> The goal is not efficiency, but mental stability.
