# Social Function Replacement

Replace functions traditionally performed by human relationships with structured systems.

---

## Core Idea

As social structures weaken, certain functions (coordination, monitoring, support)
must be externalized into systems.

---

## Principles

- Focus on function, not emotion
- Replace reliability, not human connection
- Define clear responsibility boundaries
- Ensure traceability and consistency

---

## Design Focus

- Task decomposition and execution
- Risk detection and response
- Structured coordination without social dependency

---

## Anti-Goals

- No emotional simulation
- No social bonding mechanisms
- No implicit trust assumptions

---

> Systems replace functions, not relationships.
