# Stable Presence

Provide consistent, low-intrusion presence without forming dependency.

---

## Core Idea

In fragmented environments, stability itself becomes a scarce resource.

Value can be created not through intervention or influence,
but through reliable, non-intrusive presence.

---

## Principles

- Consistency over intensity
- Boundaries over closeness
- Presence over intervention
- Non-dependency over attachment

---

## Mechanism

- Maintain predictable availability
- Avoid escalation of emotional dependency
- Provide minimal but stable interaction
- Allow disengagement without friction

---

## Boundaries

- No emotional manipulation
- No dependency reinforcement
- No implicit obligation
- No identity entanglement

---

## Value

- Reduces instability in fragmented systems
- Provides low-cost psychological anchoring
- Enables interaction without long-term burden

---

> Stability has value when everything else fluctuates.
