# Signal

Maintain a trustworthy picture of the system before action.

---

## Core Idea

Failure often begins before execution:
the state is invalid, the rule has drifted, or the record is weak.

This layer protects epistemic reliability.

---

## Scope

It asks:

- Is the current decision state valid?
- Are the rules still trustworthy?
- Is the record traceable without endorsement?

---

## Mechanisms

- Decision-state validation
- Drift detection
- Traceable recording

---

## Output

- validity warnings
- drift signals
- traceable records
- requests to delay or re-check

---

## Boundary

Signal does not:

- authorize action
- enforce steps
- allocate responsibility
- recover systems
- coordinate actors

---

> Before control, there must be signal.
