# Control

Constrain critical action so safety does not depend on trust, memory, or informal responsibility.

---

## Core Idea

Once action begins, the main problem is control:
who may act, what must be confirmed, and what cannot be skipped.

---

## Scope

It asks:

- Which actions require explicit gating?
- Which steps must be structurally enforced?
- Which tasks may be delegated without transferring liability?

---

## Mechanisms

- Human-gated action
- Non-bypassable process checkpoints
- Responsibility decoupling

---

## Output

- action gates
- confirmation checkpoints
- enforced process steps
- clean executor/owner separation

---

## Boundary

Control does not:

- determine truth
- recover degraded systems
- organize social trust
- define expression grammars

---

> Action should become harder exactly where error becomes expensive.
