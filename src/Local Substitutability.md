# Local Substitutability

Transform unavailable resources into usable alternatives under constraint.

---

## Core Idea

When supply chains fail or resources become inaccessible,
systems require the ability to locally reconstruct functionality
from limited inputs.

---

## Principles

- Function over fidelity
- Availability over optimization
- Reproducibility over perfection
- Independence from original supply chains

---

## Mechanism

- Reverse understanding of required function
- Reconstruction using available tools and materials
- Iterative refinement until usable state is reached
- Documentation for repeatability

---

## Boundaries

- No reliance on original providers
- No assumption of ideal materials or conditions
- No requirement for full equivalence

---

## Value

- Reduces systemic dependency
- Enables resilience under disruption
- Preserves operational continuity

---

> Availability beats precision when systems are under constraint.
