# Non-Endorsing Traceable Record

Provide verifiable, traceable information without making any claims about its value or outcome.

---

## Core Idea

In high-uncertainty systems, trust should not come from authority or claims,
but from raw, traceable information.

---

## Principles

- Record facts, not interpretations
- Preserve original data without modification
- Ensure traceability across the entire flow
- Separate information from endorsement

---

## Boundaries

- No evaluation of quality or effectiveness
- No recommendations or conclusions
- No assumption of responsibility beyond data integrity

---

## Design Focus

- Reduce ambiguity, not persuade
- Enable independent verification
- Maintain neutrality in all contexts

---

> Trust should emerge from visibility, not assertion.
