# Minimal System Recovery

Restore functionality in partially failed systems with minimal intervention.

---

## Core Idea

When systems fail, full reconstruction is often unnecessary and too costly.

The goal is to:
- identify the root cause across layers
- apply minimal, reversible fixes
- keep the system operational

---

## Principles

- Prioritize survival over optimization
- Prefer minimal intervention over full repair
- Ensure all fixes are reversible
- Diagnose across both software and physical layers
- Accept degraded performance if stability is restored

---

## Focus

- Hybrid failures (multiple interacting causes)
- Boundary/interface breakdowns
- Time-critical recovery scenarios

---

## Anti-Goals

- No full system redesign
- No long-term maintenance
- No assumption of ideal conditions

---

> A system does not need to be correct. It needs to stay alive.
