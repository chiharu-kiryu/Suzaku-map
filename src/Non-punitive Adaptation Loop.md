# Non-punitive Adaptation Loop

Enable gradual adaptation without punishment or reward-driven optimization.

---

## Core Idea

Systems can guide behavior not by reinforcement or penalty,
but by allowing individuals to become familiar
through repeated, low-risk interaction.

---

## Principles

- No punishment for failure
- No reward for optimization
- No pressure for progress
- Consistent system response to behavior

---

## Mechanism

- Failure resets state without loss of context
- Repeated exposure builds familiarity
- Reduced friction emerges naturally over time
- System response reflects interaction patterns, not performance

---

## Boundaries

- No gamification (points, rewards, streaks)
- No scarcity pressure
- No forced goals or progression
- No emotional manipulation

---

## Value

- Encourages long-term engagement without stress
- Supports natural learning and adaptation
- Reduces avoidance behavior under uncertainty

---

> Familiarity replaces optimization.
