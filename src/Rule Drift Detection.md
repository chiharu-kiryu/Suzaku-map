# Rule Drift Detection

Detect when previously valid rules, assumptions, or pathways are no longer reliable.

---

## Core Idea

Systems often fail not because of incorrect actions,
but because actions are based on outdated assumptions.

---

## Focus

- Changes in rules, interpretations, or enforcement
- Divergence across similar sources or systems
- Gradual disappearance of previously valid paths

---

## Principles

- Record signals, not interpretations
- Do not predict or explain
- Do not provide recommendations
- Preserve temporal context

---

## Signals

- Repeated anomalies across independent sources
- Increasing inconsistency in responses
- Silent disappearance of known patterns

---

## Anti-Goals

- No forecasting
- No guidance
- No optimization

---

> The most critical change is often the one that is not announced.
