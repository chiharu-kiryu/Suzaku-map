# Coordination

Maintain cooperation when formal systems are weak, absent, or too costly.

---

## Core Idea

When institutions fail, the missing problem is not just labor allocation.
It is continuity, trust, adaptation, and future access.

---

## Scope

It asks:

- Which social functions can be externalized into systems?
- How can collaboration persist without central backing?
- What kind of presence stabilizes interaction without dependency?
- How can adaptation happen without punishment or reward pressure?
- How can relationships preserve future optionality without ownership?

---

## Mechanisms

- Functional externalization
- Trust-based coordination
- Stable presence
- Non-punitive adaptation
- Optionality capture

---

## Output

- small coordination structures
- bounded support relationships
- low-pressure adaptation loops
- optional access relationships

---

## Boundary

Coordination does not:

- replace human intimacy
- gate critical action
- establish factual truth
- define formal expression

---

> Where institutions fail, coordination survives through bounded trust and repeatable structure.
